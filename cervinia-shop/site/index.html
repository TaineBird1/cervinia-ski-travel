The Village

A car-free alpine gem beneath the Matterhorn.

Cervinia is truly beautiful — car-free streets, friendly locals, and the mighty Matterhorn towering over it all. Varied nightlife, discos and restaurants sit alongside snowmobiling, husky sledding, tandem paragliding, heli-skiing and mountain hut dinners with a skidoo ride up and bumboarding down. Non-skiers can even ride the cable car all the way to Zermatt, Switzerland, without putting on a pair of skis.

The Ski Area

One of Europe's highest, snow-sure resorts.

Cervinia sits at the foot of the Matterhorn with the highest lifts in Europe and some of the best snow in the Alps. Skiing for every grade — genuinely easy beginner slopes and some of the longest runs in the Alps, including the Ventino, ranked among the best in the world. In summer we ski the Theodul Glacier, with 40km of slopes on offer — ski in the morning, play the 18-hole golf course in the afternoon.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What's included in the price?

Exactly what you add to your basket — direct prices, no hidden fees or booking markups. Pick only the transfers, equipment, lift passes, lessons or accommodation you actually need.

When do shared airport shuttles run?

Shared shuttles run on Saturdays from Milan Malpensa and Turin Caselle. For Bergamo and Milan Linate, or for any other day, please book a scheduled/private transfer.

Should I get a Local or International lift pass?

Start with the Local Italian-side pass — it covers Cervinia, Valtournenche and the surrounding resorts, and is cheaper. If the weather's good enough for a Zermatt day, upgrade at the lift kiosk on the day (approximately €52) rather than pre-booking one you might not use.

What's your cancellation policy, and is there a tourist tax?

Both vary by hotel — each property's exact cancellation terms, minimum stay, and per-person, per-night tourist tax (collected locally, not included in online prices) are listed under "Rates & policy details" when you select it in the Accommodation section. For transfers, equipment, passes and lessons, WhatsApp us and we'll sort out any changes directly.

Is Cervinia good for families with young kids?

Yes — ski school takes children from age 5, and there's a supervised Kidz Zone right at the base of the slopes for younger ones. The village itself is car-free, so it's easy to get around on foot with little ones.

What about excursions or activities that aren't listed here?

Just send us a WhatsApp — husky sledding, heli-skiing, mountain hut dinners with a skidoo ride up and bumboarding down, Aosta shopping days, we arrange all of it directly.

How do I get my invoice after paying?

It's available to download as a PDF straight from your confirmation page right after checkout — no need to wait for anything else.

How far is Cervinia from the airport, and how long is the transfer?

Turin Caselle is closest, around 1h45. Milan Malpensa is around 2h15, and Milan Linate around 3h. Bergamo Orio is furthest, around 3h15. Exact travel times per airport are also shown when you book your transfer in the shop.

Do I need travel insurance or a visa to visit Cervinia?

EU/Schengen-area visitors need no visa. UK, US and most other international visitors can enter visa-free for short stays under the Schengen 90-day rule — check your own government's travel advice for your passport. We strongly recommend travel insurance that covers winter sports; our optional ski accident insurance (€3/day) covers on-mountain incidents only, not trip cancellation, medical evacuation, or lost luggage.

What languages does your team speak?

English and Italian — we're a local team, so you can reach us in whichever's easier for you.
